Classic Match

"Who sees it first?" — find the one shared symbol between two cards, fast, and shout its name.

How to play?
🎯 Goal: be the fastest to find the matching picture!
  • Place two cards face-up on the table.
  • Everyone looks together.
  • The first child to spot the picture that appears on both cards shouts it out loud: "Dad!" or "Dog!"
  • Whoever shouted first wins the round! Place a new card and keep going.
🟢

The Tower

"Collect them all!" — gather as many cards as you can from the central pile.

How to play?
🎯 Goal: collect as many cards as you can from the tower in the middle.
  • Stack the whole deck in the middle of the table like a tall tower, pictures facing up.
  • Each player gets one card in hand (or on the table in front of them).
  • Look at your card and the card on top of the tower: found a matching picture? Shout its name and take the top card for yourself!
  • Now there's a new card on top of the tower, and the game keeps moving fast.
🏆 Winner: whoever builds the tallest tower of cards by the time the pile runs out!
🟠

The Well

"Throw your cards fast!" — try to be the first to get rid of every card in your hand.

How to play?
🎯 Goal: be the first to get rid of all your cards and end up with nothing left!
  • Place a single card in the middle of the table (this is "the well").
  • Deal out the rest of the cards equally among all the kids. Everyone keeps their stack face-down in front of them.
  • Everyone flips their top card at the same time.
  • See a matching picture between your card and the one in the middle? Shout the name and quickly throw your card onto the middle pile!
  • Now your card becomes the new middle card, and everyone tries to match it.
🏆 Winner: the first child to finish all the cards in their hand!
🔴

The Trio (Catch Nine)

"Picture detective" — lay out 9 cards on the table and look for one picture that appears on 3 different cards at once!

How to play?
🎯 Goal: find one picture hiding across three different cards at the same time!
  • Lay out 9 cards on the table in a neat grid (3 rows of 3 cards).
  • Everyone looks at the nine cards together.
  • Look for a picture that appears on a first card, a second card, and a third card, all at once!
  • Found it? Shout the name of the picture, point to the 3 cards, and win them!
  • The player who took the cards replaces them with 3 new cards from the pile.
🏆 Winner: whoever searches best and collects the highest number of cards!
